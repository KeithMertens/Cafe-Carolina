Rooted in Cavite warmth, crafted for lingering.

Cafe Carolina was built as a serene garden escape just off the main Tagaytay-Mendez highway. Designed with natural timber, garden greenery, and cozy shaded alcoves, it offers respite from the city line.

Here, nothing is rushed. We pour single-origin Cavite and Batangas beans to order, bake our savory tarts fresh every dawn, and keep open tables under the trees for conversations that stretch into the afternoon.

Batangas & Cavite Single Origin
Deep, rich Barako and Liberica dark roasts sourced directly from nearby high-elevation growers.
Al-Fresco Garden Nooks
Shaded outdoor tables surrounded by native flora and cool upland air year-round.
