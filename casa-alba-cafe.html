Built slowly, by hand, for staying a while.

Casa Alba began as a family’s old rest house on the ridge. We kept the tall windows, the uneven wooden floors and the long table where everyone ends up sitting together.

Nothing here is in a hurry. Coffee is poured to order, pastries come out warm, and regulars still get the same corner chair.
